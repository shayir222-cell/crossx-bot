## Why nothing passed

Each candidate fails for at least one of these reasons:

1. **Negative expectancy** (E_norm = 0): MUSDT, AVAXUSDT, ICPUSDT, CCUSDT, BGBUSDT, XLMUSDT, LTCUSDT, XMRUSDT
2. **Structural L/S asymmetry** (B_norm = 0 or near zero → hard cap 50):
   - BCHUSDT: 0 longs (100% short generation) — pure regime artifact
3. **Insufficient edge after slippage haircut**:
   - SUIUSDT: raw +0.140R looks promising but adj_exp drops to -0.020R after tier-10 slippage subtraction → fails +0.10R anti-overfit floor
   - LINKUSDT, UNIUSDT: +0.021R / +0.025R — barely positive, all lost to slippage

## Notable structural findings

- **L/S imbalance is systemic** across this batch too. 9 of 13 pairs are >70% one-sided. Strategy v2 scoring continues to be directionally biased per asset.
- **Score model still doesn't discriminate** above MIN_SCORE=92: avg winner score ≈ avg loser score (97.x) across all pairs in this batch. Same finding as top-10 ([project_score_model_flat_above_92](../.claude/projects/c--Users--------crossx-bot/memory/project_score_model_flat_above_92.md)).
- **BGBUSDT** is catastrophic (-0.883R, DD 231%) — Bitget's own exchange token has very different behavior than tradable crypto; never deploy.
- **ICPUSDT** has unusually balanced L/S (57/42) but only 121 trades in 30d → low signal density, may need a different score threshold.

## Caveats

- Backtest is optimistic upper bound (no slippage beyond fixed fees, no MTF/news gates beyond ADX).
- Per-pair `MIN_ATR_PCT` was NOT custom-tuned — used the strategy default 0.0005 for new pairs. Tighter floors derived from each pair's own ATR distribution could lift some candidates.
- TP_R sweep not run; only TP_R=2.0 tested per scope. SUI or LINK might be more viable at TP_R=2.5 or 1.5.
- AVAXUSDT and ICPUSDT had network errors during fetch (Bitget timeouts) — replay completed on partial data; results may be 5-10% noisier than other pairs.
